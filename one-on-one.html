What it is

Structured conversation. Perspective. Next steps.

This is a focused session for people who want an honest, uncluttered conversation about the direction of their life or work.

Katlego is not a therapist, and this is not medical, psychological, legal or financial advice. It is a serious room to think clearly — with someone who will take your situation seriously and help you leave with a clearer sense of what to do next.

Common areas people bring

  • Direction and life transitions
  • Discipline and personal standards
  • Relationships and boundaries
  • Decision-making under pressure
  • Purpose, work and meaning
  • Building something you own

What you leave with

  • A clearer read of the situation
  • Language to describe what is actually happening
  • A short list of practical next steps

Boundaries

What this is not.

× Therapy or counselling
× Medical or psychological advice
× Financial or legal advice
× Emergency or crisis support

If you are in crisis, please contact a qualified professional or local emergency service.
